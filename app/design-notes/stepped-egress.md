# Corridor / demising-wall / escape-stair study

- Gallery extents use orthogonal unit-edge cuts and polygon unions instead of Voronoi cuts.
- Triplex entry/facade notches and random horizontal recesses are removed; the consistent ground-floor facade setback is retained. Retail carving remains.
- Shared boundaries produce one 8-inch opaque wall centered on the unit boundary. Glazing is excluded from those faces. Exposed returns above lower neighbors remain opaque.
- Upper-ring wings have paired-gallery landings, longitudinal rising flights, low-end return flights to the L4 terrace, inclined steel stringers and crossbeams. No new stair columns occupy the valley.
- Shared walls participate in structural geometry and load inventory. Stair supports are also visible in coordinated structure and drawings.

Validation of default geometry: 752 shared wall segments, 68 stair-flight objects, 384 support members; zero empty polygons, zero triplex notches, zero new stair columns and zero flight run/rise geometry warnings. Browser model and structural drawing overlay rendered without page errors or invalid SVG coordinates. Structural reaction ledger closes within 1e-8 kip for the modeled loads.

This is schematic geometry, NOT a code-compliant egress or final structural design certification. Gallery/stair clear widths, unit access, independent exit separation, facade fire exposure, rated enclosure requirements, guards/handrails, travel distances, L4-to-monumental-stair continuity and discharge to public way must be reviewed. Stair flight load take-down, wall/slab bearing, steel connections, deflection, vibration, foundations, fire resistance and reinforcement are not solved. The structural workspace explicitly distinguishes modeled landing loads from unsolved stair-flight support design. Existing model MEP clashes are not claimed resolved by this change.
